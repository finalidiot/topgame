# 002C.6 starter preview cleanup

Breaker and Bastion's permanent translucent preview marks are removed. Breaker
previously drew three fixed streaks outside its native silhouette; Bastion drew
two fixed blue side brackets. Both remained visible when the preview was idle
and while its blade spun. Their native steel/enamel silhouettes now carry the
identity without those detached marks. Vane's accepted orbit dots are preserved.

## Native source audit

No Aseprite modification or asset repaint was required. The artifact came from
`TopPreview._draw_identity_motion`, rather than the production master.

`assets/source-art/starter_blade_accents_002b1.aseprite` remains the editable
source used by the three runtime sheets in `assets/top/starters/`. A native
Aseprite 1.3.18.6 export, the source-reader composite and the shipped PNGs match
exactly in RGBA for all 24 keys. Every visible sheet pixel has alpha 255; every
other pixel has alpha 0. Each native cell forms one connected silhouette under
8-connectivity. The source contains no detached translucent ghost pixels.

The native master preserves three normal image layers: steel silhouette, enamel
plates and rotational rim signature bands. Breaker, Bastion and Vane retain
eight 48×48 spin keys each, pivot `[24,40]`, and per-key durations of 65, 160 and
100 ms respectively. All eight keys for each identity remain distinct. The base
rotational master and all starter source/runtime hashes are unchanged.

## Scope and verification

The production change removes only TopPreview's permanent Breaker/Bastion
identity-motion branches. The Vane orbit geometry, alpha and clock formula are
unchanged. Native before/after rendering confirms **zero changed Vane pixels**
across both captured preview states. The Breaker/Bastion part stack, shadow,
opaque mechanical hub, spin cadence and body rhythms remain intact.

Actual native 640×360 gameplay was inspected at idle, spin, movement, real
wobble and accepted impact for all three starters. These captures use legal
starter assemblies, real countdown/launch and steering/burst/brake inputs from
the existing bot. No position, velocity, RPM, wobble, contact or outcome was
injected, and no collection save was opened. Bastion's naturally stable wobble
sample is approximately 0.156; the other two samples exceed 0.5. The precise
state values accompany the captures.

The same fifteen input-driven gameplay states were captured again after the
preview correction. Every recorded physical state and every gameplay pixel
outside the before/after caption is identical. This confirms the correction
does not spill into Battle's presentation or mechanics.

Battle does not call the preview helper. Its real burst-gated smear and contact
effects remain unchanged. No separate Battle correction, physics/stat change,
procedural replacement overlay or transparent cutout backfill was needed.

The rendered regression compares Breaker/Bastion against the actual assembly
renderer with identity marks suppressed at three clocks, in idle and animated
modes. It also checks Vane's visible orbit/normal opaque occlusion, nearest
filtering, the legal assemblies, native dimensions/pivot and protected art
hashes. Run it with a real rendering driver:

```powershell
godot --path . --script res://tests/test_starter_preview_cleanup.gd --audio-driver Dummy
```

The headless dummy renderer cannot read rendered pixels. This focused test uses
native rendering and passed 117 checks with zero failures.
