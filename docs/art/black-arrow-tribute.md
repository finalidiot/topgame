# Black Arrow tribute direction

Human requirement: one beast manifestation must tribute PAC / Neville
(Benjamin Satterley), specifically the Black Arrow. The first manifestation pass
implements this apparition on existing Smash, Lopsider and Fork Blades. It
preserves the 31-part catalogue and existing mechanical parameters. The new
manifestation checkpoint awaits human visual review; earlier milestone status
is recorded separately in `docs/tasks/002C.5-milestone-acceptance.md`.

## Identity and movement

Use **Black Arrow** as a Blade identity with a **ghostlike human silhouette**
performing PAC's Black Arrow. The human's latest direction supersedes the earlier
bird proposal. Use a similar compact, muscular body shape: broad shoulders,
a powerful chest, a narrow waist and strong thighs, with a short trailing hair
shape where readable. Recognition should come from proportions and movement;
facial detail is unnecessary at gameplay scale.

The Blade supplies the apparition; the equipped ability supplies the action;
ability upgrades control how fully the creature appears. Ordinary parts and
powers remain compatible, without a new class restriction.

The tribute's defining sequence is **gather -> rise -> corkscrew -> descend ->
impact -> recover**. It needs both the backward somersault and axial twist of
the shooting-star movement. Ordinary spindle rotation alone cannot communicate
that action. Show a compressed takeoff pose, an unmistakable backward arch,
a tucked corkscrew and an opening into the chest-down landing. During the twist,
one shoulder and arm should cross ahead of the other so the rotation reads.
The silhouette should reproduce the move's body action, with the ghost gathering from the top
and dissolving back into it during recovery.

Use a smoky charcoal interior, a pale silver/grey edge and a restrained translucent
trail to establish its ghostlike presence. One main apparition carries the pose.
Preserve the negative spaces between arms, torso and legs so the figure reads
as a person through the twist. Keep the
hub and actual top readable, rendering the apparition behind the opaque top;
transparency must not erase the body's key poses or conceal the machine.

An eventual dedicated Blade could have a compact physical hub and two swept striking lobes,
with clear negative spaces. Its silhouette must still read as a spinning-top
component. Charcoal metal and silver edge highlights keep it legible on the
arena floor; colour alone cannot identify it. No arrow-shaped projectile.

## Gameplay and presentation constraints

- Precision and commitment define its fantasy: strong aligned contact, limited
  correction after commitment, and a meaningful recovery cost after a miss.
  Existing equipped powers supply those mechanics; this visual pass adds no
  numerical bonus or new aerial collision system.
- Keep the actual top silhouette and its floor shadow visible. The apparition's
  motion must follow the player's real path and strike, with the impact pose
  and shockwave driven by actual contact. A miss must produce a miss/recovery
  pose, without a fabricated target reaction or impact flash.
- A real airborne top would require bounded elevation/contact rules. Do not
  imply an implemented jump, grant free invulnerability or fake a mechanical
  aerial attack by moving only the sprite. The apparition can rise while the
  top stays in the current physical model, with that distinction kept clear.
- Partial manifestations may use a brief shoulder/arm outline or a tucked
  turning pose; full giant emergence belongs to a major committed trigger.
  Preserve the broad corkscrew/descent
  action using 4-8 deliberate poses, rather than a continuous opaque screen fill.
- Inspect native 640x360 nearest-neighbour gameplay, grayscale silhouette,
  missed attacks and crowded battles before accepting the effect. Do not
  overwrite Iron Comet or silently change provisional C5 powers to add it.

Suggested collection description: **"A compact striker that commits
everything to one twisting impact. Miss, and the landing is yours to survive."**
