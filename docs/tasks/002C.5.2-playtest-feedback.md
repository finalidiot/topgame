## Movement, overdrive and route limits

Drift is grounded player handling, not an extra enemy AI speed benefit. It needs
actual steering and lateral slip, speed above 65, meaningful reserve and floor
contact. The friction change restores at most 64% of the removed lateral
component multiplied by bounded slide intensity; it does not add kinetic energy.
Higher-grip components still resist it. Sparks use the cosmetic RNG, fade in
0.14–0.24 seconds and share the existing 120-particle budget. Brake-held Orbit
Drive receives a sparks-only fallback; its prior drift friction is preserved.

In the focused turn probe, Ball's mean speed was 174.81 versus 156.20 without
the correction, while Chisel's strong side scrub kept its difference small.
Those fixed probes demonstrate the intended handling contrast, not a human
verdict about every controller or assembly.

Redline mechanics remain the existing real-reserve system. Its active caps are
1.12 rank I, 1.18 rank II/default III and 1.24 Runaway, corresponding to 10,080,
10,620 and 11,160 RPM. Motion earns at most 0.045 reserve/second from a 0.14
activation allowance; valid impacts earn at most 0.055 through existing economy
budgets. Activation and heat still spend reserve, and expiry vents earned excess
through accounted spending. The HUD now displays that existing reality.

Modern Afterimage emission remains paid and movement-dependent. Rank I retains
at most 26 traces, deeper non-Ghost routes at most 36, Ghost Circuit at most 40,
with the existing 72-trace global cap. Ghost Circuit's six-second lifetime,
actual connected closure geometry and paid-trigger requirement are preserved.
No invisible chord, free redraw or fake target reaction was introduced.

## Dead Centre mechanics

The existing controlled position rule charges the anchor inside radius 115 while
speed is at most 85 and the player is braking or steering gently. Full hold starts
at charge 0.70 and matures over six seconds. Aggression, burst, departure or an
extreme physical hit releases the relevant brace state.

Actual recovery and inward gravity additionally require the inner centre:
radius 58, speed at most 55, a live reserve above spin-out and a controlled full
hold. The new gain source is `dead_centre`, paid through the existing `gain_rpm`
and shared economy budget. Baseline running costs and elimination rewards were
not retuned.

| Mature inward pull radius | 104 | 124 | 124 |
| Maximum inward acceleration offer | 32 world units/s² | 46 world units/s² | 46 world units/s² |

Recovery is paid in approximately half-second instalments, consuming only the
actual amount granted. A genuine rank investment adds its extra capacity once.
Hits, charge loss, burst and passive return cannot replenish the quota. Rearming
requires 1.25 seconds of deliberate unbraked steering of at least 0.35, speed of
at least 35 and position outside radius 82. The HUD exposes seek, hold, actual
eligible recovery and exhausted-quota rearming states.

The incoming-loss hook affects actual full-top and small-top received RPM and
wobble shock without increasing outgoing attack or removing contact damage.
Counterweight still stores incoming force and spends it through its existing
Burst retaliation; this pass does not replace that mutation.

The inward force uses the real anchor socket, distance falloff, target mass
resistance, dt-scaled offers and a 16-target budget. It stops offering extra
acceleration at inward speed 120 and clamps each dt offer to at most 0.10 seconds.
Bosses additionally receive a 0.45 force factor. Allies and targets outside the
finite radius are unaffected. Floor arcs follow actual pull radius and maturity.

## Temporary rerolls and planned budget

Each Run begins with one reroll charge and holds at most six. A valid reroll
spends one, changes at least one eligible offered card, retains the existing
development-slot guarantee and deterministically revisions the offer. Stale
seed/draft/revision callbacks cannot consume or claim it again. Rerolling is
disabled during mutation selection or when no alternative card is eligible.

Real threat clears 1, 4, 7 and every subsequent third clear, plus boss clears, can
spawn a flat floor chip. At most two coexist, expire after 180 seconds of advancing
battle time and grant one charge on actual swept player contact within radius 14.
Pause/drafts freeze them; a full charge inventory refuses collection. Run restart
clears temporary charges and chips. This state is absent from permanent ownership
saves.

The human clarified that a future budget allowing several upgrades and reroll
spending belongs **within the Run**. That budget is planned only. See
[Run upgrade budget scope](../architecture/run-upgrade-budget.md).
