## Diagnosis and minimum correction

Only one production value changes: Bastion `handling.spin_drain`, **0.90 → 1.00**.
Mass **1.35**, bank **1.35**, recovery **1.30**, acceleration **0.72**, speed **0.78**,
impact **1.00**, orbit **0.25**, and Guard / Low / Ball remain exact. No power,
enemy, collision solver, catalogue or universal economy value changes. The
same starter coefficient is used on Windows and Android.

Guard / Low / Ball derive stamina **8.7**, stability **9.7**, mass rating **9.2**,
grip **5.2**, speed **2.7**, power **4.0**. They are legacy-compatible parts:
`part_physics.enabled` is false for this assembly. Their real stat-derived mass,
stamina and stability matter; modern custom contour/inertia/centre-hold factors
must not be incorrectly credited to this trio. Neutral base mass is **8.952**;
Bastion handling makes it **12.0852** before temporary defensive modifiers.

The exact ordinary passive cost is
`max(0.0008, (0.0032 - stamina * 0.00022) * handling.spin_drain * power_drain)`.
For this trio with power drain 1, removing the duplicated 10% discount raises
cost from **0.0011574** to **0.001286** full reserve per combat second: **3.858
percentage points** more reserve spent over 300 seconds. Activity does not switch
this cost; there is no new AFK penalty, death timer or special drain source.

The observed comfort is a temporary plateau, not a reproduced infinite economic
equilibrium. After recent-control eligibility and finite recovery are exhausted,
accepted main already has strictly negative no-input economics. Full Bulwark can
multiply effective anchoring mass by **81**, reaching **978.9012**, with incoming
RPM scale **0.45**; a subsequent Crash Guard II window can reduce that to **0.216**.
Rank II/III without Bulwark reaches **16 times** base effective mass at full
charge and maturity; charging and received impacts lower instantaneous values.
Strong automatic wobble relief, instant Bulwark wobble reduction and bank return
can leave position visibly comfortable while RPM is deteriorating.

At full Bulwark, ordinary stat/recovery/power wobble relief can total approximately
**0.67054 per second**, plus **0.06** per qualifying received hit. Dead Centre's
finite **0.28** recovery quota can mask up to roughly **242 seconds of old passive
cost alone**, before contact costs; it does not continuously refill while AFK.
The old seed-421 legacy-Bulwark mature window spent **0.34724** passive and
**0.51189** collision reserve, recovered **0.22228** from the remaining quota,
and ended at **35.08%**. Its tiny movement/wobble costs explain the impression of
effortless centre hold. The candidate ends that same declared scenario at
**26.48%**, with intermittent defence at **96.85%**. Different warmup handoffs and
later physical contacts contribute to this outcome difference; only the direct
passive increment above is an isolated guaranteed coefficient effect.

Single-factor initial handling ablations also examined bank and recovery.
Reducing all three had little additional mature loss beyond the spin change and
reduced active reserve more. Those experiments modify handling once at opening,
so their later trajectories include different handoffs/admissions. They are
diagnostics, not same-state causal final-RPM estimates. The smallest supported
correction retains Bastion's weight, recovery and return. No new brace subsystem
or AI change was necessary.
