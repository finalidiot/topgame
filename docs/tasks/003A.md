## Permanent progression

Run → earned CREDITS → Results → optional Shop → physical Parts Packet →
NEW designs / duplicate SALVAGE → Workshop → another machine and Run.

The two permanent currencies are **CREDITS** and **SALVAGE**. SALVAGE deliberately
avoids the existing Ratchet name SCRAP. No real-money purchases, premium currency,
advertising, daily deals, countdowns, rotating offers or monetisation hooks exist.
Ownership means access to one design; duplicate quantities are never accumulated.
The existing 31 parts, six rarity identities and 1,089 legal assemblies retain
their physics and ratings. Exactly one starter machine grants its three parts.
Other starter designs are ordinary eligible packet content; no permanent class lock.

## Run payout

`packet_economy.json` is the production reward and price source:

`min(600, 12 × earned threat clears + 10 × earned elite clears + 30 × earned boss clears)`.

Each eligible elite/boss also counts as a threat clear. No money comes from time,
tiny collisions, raw damage, Run XP or AFK survival. ContinuousRun accounts for
player-attributed eliminations with actual recent steering, braking or Burst; a swarm requires
three attributed small eliminations, rather than expiry/cleanup. Fine analogue
steering above the existing deadzone is accepted. Aborts/restarts pay zero.
Explicit threat/event fixtures invalidate economic eligibility. Practice,
catalogue QA and smoke flows cannot receive normal gameplay payouts.

RunRewards verifies seed, distinct serials, actual completed loss and the live
earned-clear ledger. Specialist threats are included. CollectionSave allocates a
persisted monotonic Run nonce at genuine launch; settlement credits it once.
Repeated Results/callbacks/reloads cannot repay a nonce. A transient payout write
failure retains the verified in-memory outcome for RETRY CREDITS; navigation
cannot discard it. At the wallet cap Shop spending makes room before retry.
An explicit progression reset retires this session outcome and nonce too.

Played development samples: defensive two Runs earned 24 + 24 CREDITS; hybrid
and aggressive samples earned 60 in one Run. The final video uses five earned
clears and a natural ring-out at 106.97 seconds: 60 CREDITS, not a UI grant.

## Products, odds and recycling

| Packet | Price | Contents and guarantee |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| PARTS PACKET | 48 CREDITS | One Blade, one Ratchet, one Bit; at least one Uncommon+ |
| RECLAIMED PARTS PACKET | 36 SALVAGE | Same three categories and Uncommon+; at least one unowned eligible design while any remain |

Base rarity weights are TRASH 7, COMMON 50, UNCOMMON 28, RARE 10, EPIC 4,
LEGENDARY 1. A standard packet uniformly selects an eligible category for its
Uncommon+ slot; remaining slots use base pools. Unavailable rarity/category
combinations are excluded and the available positive weights normalized. Parts
within a rarity are uniform. Empty categories/impossible guarantees refuse
purchase rather than use a hidden fallback.

Reclaimed chooses uniformly among categories with missing eligible designs,
then weights rarities conditioned on that category's missing pool. A different
eligible category supplies Uncommon+. Remaining contents may duplicate. A
complete collection uses standard rules and does not claim NEW. The Info panel
provides standard odds, actual ownership-conditioned Reclaimed odds, and
recycling values directly from the production sampler/configuration.

Every current ordinary part has explicit `pack_eligible` and
`acquisition_source` metadata. Future boss/signature parts can be excluded.
No boss drops or two-player gameplay are implemented.

packet/Run serials, one pending packet, last receipt and last reward guards.
Schema 0/1 migration preserves starter, ownership and equipped build, with zero
new economic balances; independent preference files are untouched. Unknown
future schema versions remain refused. Corruption recovery, stale-write
protection, neighbouring verified temporary writes and backup safety remain.

Purchase verifies funds/pools → rolls exact three results and NEW/duplicate
conversion once → atomically persists debit plus fixed pending receipt →
idempotently commits ownership/SALVAGE → presents the animation. The resolved
pending receipt stays saved until inspection is acknowledged. Restart recovers
the same pending/resolved receipt; it never rerolls. Animation contains no
economic mutation. Stale confirmations, replayed finalization and repeated
acknowledgement cannot charge/grant again. Overflow checks account for the
Reclaimed SALVAGE debit before its bounded duplicate return.

wallets, receipt/counters/reward guards and the session's unpaid outcome.
