# Future combat content — human notes after 003A.1

These are directions for a later content milestone. They are **not implemented
by the 003A.1 acceptance correction**, and they do not authorize 003B or a save
migration.

- Add more aura powers with a distinct physical job, readable boundaries and a
  useful player decision. Avoid passive damage fields that reward standing still.
- Expand cross-power synergies that change how a cost is managed. One mechanism
  should create an opportunity to release, reposition, counter or pay another
  mechanism's cost. Do not reduce synergy to stacked multipliers.
- Add more bosses. Each needs its own silhouette, brighter special palette,
  bespoke entrance, signature physical behavior and identifiable signature parts.
  Arena entrances and simultaneous encounters must remain readable at 640×360.
- Make beast-bearing blades special discoveries. Future starter machines should
  begin without Beast Blades. Some Rare+ blades may bear a beast; many should not.
  Earn discoveries through progression, packets and boss systems. Design the
  catalogue, collection migration and existing-player treatment together before
  changing current blade ownership or the accepted Blade → Beast mapping.

The current automatic beast is still rare punctuation for an exceptional physical
collision. A future earned **Beast Charge → MANIFEST READY → dedicated input →
physics-driven signature special** is separate. Its existing design is in
[BEAST_MANIFEST_FUTURE.md](BEAST_MANIFEST_FUTURE.md). An automatic extreme-hit
manifestation does not currently consume a charge meter; no charge meter, button,
special-move state or save field is introduced in 003A.1.

Windows, controller and Android landscape should share one readable action
language. Reserve final bindings when the special is designed; do not displace
Burst or Brake. Larger aura, synergy and boss rosters need their own balance and
performance review. Multiplayer remains deferred to its own milestone.
