# Beast manifestation review

Use `builds/latest/SpinningMetal.exe` for the normal game. The new apparitions
follow your Blade and major equipped powers. Hover a Blade in the workshop to
see its spirit name. Changing Ratchet or Bit retains the Blade's identity.

For an immediate PAC tribute test, run
`tools/playtest/windows/QA-Black-Arrow.cmd`. This opens an isolated Breaker/Smash
practice with existing Breakneck powers; its collection/preferences path is
separate from your real save. Press Burst to activate overdrive, then Burst
again while active to commit the strike. A wall rebound with Iron Comet can
also summon the charged Black Arrow sequence. Hit a rival to resolve its
landing; a miss should dissolve without an impact.

| Blade | Spirit | Useful test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smash, Lopsider, Fork | Black Arrow | Iron Comet wall rebound into contact; paid Breakneck commitment. |
| Hammerfall, Sawtooth, Puck | Iron Bull | A meaningful Impact Wake collision. |
| Guard | Stone Tortoise | Dead Centre: brake near the middle and sustain a mature hold. |
| Balance, Outrigger, Hook, Crescent | Coil Dragon | Charged Comet or paid Breakneck; inspect curved movement and miss recovery. |

Every Blade can use those powers. The ghost supplies visual identity; the
equipped ability still supplies its normal mechanics. This checkpoint keeps
part ratings, power balance and ownership intact.

Review at native gameplay size: can you recognise the human's backward arch
and tucked corkscrew, the bull's horned head, the tortoise's planted shell and
the dragon's curved body? The real top should remain visible throughout. Watch
busy fights and pause/resume as well as clean examples. Please flag apparitions
that feel too faint, too frequent, detached from contact or hard to identify.

The labelled gameplay showcase and native/grayscale pose sheets are stored
under `GyroBrothers-QA/002C.5.2/`. Their exact paths and checkpoint build are
recorded in `manifests/002c5_2_beasts_checkpoint.json`.
