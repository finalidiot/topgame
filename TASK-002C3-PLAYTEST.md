# Task 002C.3 — RPM survival checkpoint

Run `releases/windows/SpinningMetal.exe`. Choose START RUN, Breaker/Bastion/Vane,
and a starting power. Launch once and keep fighting. No developer commands are
needed. WASD/arrows steer, Space Bursts, Shift brakes, Esc pauses. Controller:
left stick, bottom face button Burst/Confirm, shoulder/trigger brake, Start pause.
Release controls after a draft to return to combat.

RPM is now actually spent. Moving hard, Burst, powers, instability, wall strikes
and enemy hits cost reserve. Controlled movement conserves it. Meaningful hits
and recently credited kills return some spin; elites/bosses can give larger partial
returns. Tiny taps, stationary contacts and dense small kills cannot refill freely.
Look for green **+RPM RECLAIM**, red **LOW SPIN**, and the distinct **SECOND WIND**
label. Second Wind remains once per launch. Bosses and waves never refill/reset you.

Try aggressive Breaker, controlled Bastion, then mobile Vane; cross-build powers
are still available. Compare a missed Burst with a successful high-speed impact.
Try conserving spin between threats without letting yourself drift into a gate.
Reach low RPM, attempt a comeback, fight an elite/boss, and continue until defeat.
The director is unchanged. There is no game duration limit.

## Questions

1. Does RPM feel like a survival resource, and can you tell why it drops?
2. Can successful combat save a dying Run? Does that recovery feel earned?
3. Is aggression dangerous but sustainable when successful?
4. Does defence conserve spin without feeling immortal or boring?
5. Do Burst, Redline and mutations still feel costly?
6. Does low RPM create readable tension? Does Second Wind still matter?
7. Does spin-out feel influenced by your choices?
