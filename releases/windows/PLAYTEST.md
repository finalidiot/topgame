# Task 002C.5 human checkpoint

Open `releases/windows/SpinningMetal.exe` → START RUN → starter → starting power.
One launch continues until defeat. Keyboard: WASD/arrows, Space Burst, Shift
brake, Esc pause. Controller: left stick, bottom face button, shoulder/trigger,
Start. Release inputs after a draft to rearm combat.

Thirteen families are available to every starter. Seven family slots keep
room for hybrids; later drafts develop the installed powers. Try Redline on
Breaker, Dead Centre on Bastion, Afterimage on Vane, then cross those pairings.
Acquire Rank II and a mutation normally. `QA-*.cmd` launchers are optional
opening presets; danger and power activations still need real play.

With Orbit Drive, enter at speed, hold brake and turn into an arc. Carry the
curve to build flow; sharp reversals break it. Try High Gear for a faster slide,
or Momentum Bank to release deliberate straight braking into the next Burst.
Does this carry a satisfying drift, and can you choose the line?
