TASK 002B.1 / IMPOSSIBLE MECHANICAL PHENOMENA

power_cards_002b1.aseprite is the editable production master for the
new power-card illustrations: 64x64 pixels, 36 frames, five normal RGBA
layers, one embedded named 12-colour palette and a contact_pivot slice
at (32,32). All cels are deliberately authored integer geometry.
No filtered/downsampled painting, image-generation output or downloaded
art is used. Pixel clusters and plate edges stay sharp at native scale.

Layers (bottom to top):
industrial backplate
machine depth and chassis
authored moving bearings
pressure and trajectory
contact glints and fragments

Six forward-loop tags, each six frames, in the stable active-ID order:
impact_wake 0-5: expanding toothed cyan pressure race, white contact core.
second_wind 6-11: fragmented steel bearing contracts, aligns and releases.
redline 12-17: overloaded deep rotor, jagged hot teeth, opposed white arcs.
iron_comet 18-23: heavy steel wedge follows an angular wall rebound path.
afterimage 24-29: original solid rotor splits into staggered hollow echoes.
chain_impact 30-35: pressure detonations travel between three real rotors.

Frame timing per tag is 110,90,75,75,100,170 milliseconds (620ms loop).
The longer last pose gives each phenomenon a readable resting beat.
Only the focused reward card loops; non-focused cards hold a readable
mechanical pose. Acquisition reuses the same authored sequence.

Export assets/powers/cards.png uses six columns: 384x384, 64px cells,
one power per row. assets/powers/manifest.json records all layer names,
tag spans, frame durations and pivot. RunPowerCatalog exposes matching
card metadata and very short category/copy. Chain Impact copy includes
both knockout chains and the existing heavy-hit-to-Burst duel rule.

power_icons_002b.aseprite is revised at 16x16 native pixels with six
dedicated compact mechanical glyphs. It is hand-authored independently,
not a downsample of card art. Its six legacy IDs/frames, 96x16 export
and pivot remain compatible with the combat HUD and power visuals.

EDIT / EXPORT
1. Open and edit the master .aseprite. Keep normal RGBA layers, six
   stable ID tags, canvas size and contact_pivot convention.
2. Run python tools/build_power_art.py to export edited source pixels.
   This reads sources, exports losslessly and never reauthors cels.
3. Import in Godot. Inspect native 640x360 and 2x integer output.
   Draw at integer positions with nearest filtering, no mipmaps.

python tools/build_power_art.py --author-cards deliberately rebuilds
ONLY the card master and compact icon master from the initial authored
geometry. It overwrites those two sources; do not run after artist edits.
The combat power_fx_002b and small_top_002b sources remain untouched.
The older --author switch still reconstructs the three original 002B
masters and must not be used for an ordinary artist export.
